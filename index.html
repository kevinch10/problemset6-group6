KartikIn one slideProblem Set 6, in plain words

Two computer helpers, and one person who decides

1 · The writer

Every morning, a computer program reads the companies’ new results and writes a short summary.

→

2 · The checker

A second program checks that summary against the official report, like a teacher marking homework.

→

3 · A person

Reads both before the 8:30 meeting, looks at anything marked, and makes the decision.

AI people call these helpers “agents”. Ours ran by themselves every morning while we slept.

1

They save time

The helpers did the dull reading and checking every morning, on their own.

2

They only check what they see

When the writer left something out, like a one-time cost, the checker could not notice it.

3

A person still decides

A short check every morning, and a settings check every week, by the person who presents.

Let the computer do the reading. Keep a person for the judgment.
